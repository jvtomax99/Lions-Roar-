THE LINEAGE

From Thai boxing’s golden era to the Bronx.

In the 1980s, the Sor.Thanikul camp in Bangkok was one of the most powerful gyms in Muay Thai. Its fighters included legends like Dieselnoi Chor Thanasukarn, Bangkhlanoi and Boonlai Sor.Thanikul. That is the school of Muay Thai Kru William learned in 1989, and the one he still teaches today: the traditional Thai method, respect first, technique always.

ROUND 03 · CHAMPIONS

Home of champions.

Belts don’t come from a single class. At Lion’s Roar they come from the same four things Kru William has taught since 1990.

  1. The Thai methodTraining built on how Muay Thai is taught in Thailand, passed down from the Sor.Thanikul camp in Bangkok.
  2. Respect firstCourtesy and discipline before technique. The Wai Kru before every fight honors that bond between fighter and teacher.
  3. Real ring timeThrough Lion’s Roar Promotions, Kru William puts his fighters on real cards against gyms from as far as Virginia. Experience is built in the ring, not just the gym.
  4. Every level, every ageChampions here started as beginners in the same room, from kids’ classes to the fight team.
